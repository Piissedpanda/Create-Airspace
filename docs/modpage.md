# Create: Airspace

![Banner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/banner.jpg)

**Aviation and airport accessories for Create.** Build a proper airfield around your aircraft: paint runway markings, handle luggage with Create's logistics, set up barrier posts, terminal seats, terminal lights, check-in desks with luggage scales, hangar doors, taxiway signs, edge lights, a windsock that follows the wind and a rotating airport beacon.

Made for aviation builds, Create: Airspace sits right next to Create and Create Aeronautics. It uses Create's own processes, Ponder scenes and wrench handling, so everything feels like part of Create.

---

## Barrier Posts

![Barrier Posts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/barrier_posts.jpg)

Connect two posts with a wrench and a retractable belt stretches between them. Each post holds several belts, perfect for queue lines. Recolor belts with dye.
Available in **industrial iron, andesite, brass, copper and train casing**.

## Terminal Seats

![Terminal Seats](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/terminal_seats.jpg)

Seats facing the same way join into rows, with armrests only at the ends. Sit down like on Create's seats, leashed mobs too, and they keep working on contraptions. 16 colors, recolor with dye.

## Airfield Signs

![Airfield Signs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/airfield_signs.jpg)

Location, direction and mandatory signs. Write on a whole row with a named **name tag**, a **clipboard** or a **Display Link**. The text spans the row, glows at night and is readable from both sides. Switch the sign type with a wrench.

## Edge Lights

![Edge Lights](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/edge_lights.jpg)

Light up with redstone. Redstone dust connects to them, so a single line beside a row of lights powers all of them. White, blue, green, red and yellow.

## Windsock

![Windsock](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/windsock.jpg)

Turns into the wind and rises with its strength. The wind is the same for every player and gets stronger with rain, thunderstorms and height.

## Airport Beacon

![Airport Beacon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/airport_beacon.jpg)

Rotational force turns the head, redstone from the sides controls brightness and range of the light. Switch between the patterns of **land airports, water airports, heliports and military airports** with a wrench. Looking straight into a beam dazzles you for a moment (can be turned off in the client config).

## Runway Paint

<!-- Screenshot: painted runway with center line, threshold bars, numbers and a yellow holding position line -->

Fill a **Paint Pail** with white or yellow paint from a spout or a tank and paint center lines, edge lines, threshold bars, holding position lines, curves and **2x3 runway numbers** (0-9, L, R, C). Sneak-right-click switches the pattern. Paint is mixed from dye, a clay ball and water.

## Luggage

<!-- Screenshot: suitcase packager sending suitcases along a chain conveyor, baggage cart loaded with luggage -->

The **Suitcase Packager** works like Create's packager, but packs **suitcases, trolleys, duffel bags and backpacks**. Luggage has addresses, rides frogports and hangs from chain conveyors by its handle. Load it onto a **Baggage Cart**: carts join into groups of up to 3x3 with one shared inventory and fit on your vehicles.

## Ground Crew

<!-- Screenshot: player waving marshalling wands next to wheel chocks, wearing ear defenders -->

**Marshalling wands** wave while you hold right-click, **ear defenders** muffle loud sounds (also in the Curios head slot) and **wheel chocks** sit in front of the wheels.

## Terminal Lights

<!-- Screenshot: terminal hall with colored light panels on the ceiling, light strips along the walls and floor lights -->

**Light panels** join into lit ceilings and walls, **light strips** into lines, **floor lights** sit flat in the floor. Without a signal they stay off, redstone dims them up to full, and right-click keeps them always on. Dye recolors them without being used up. With **Opal Light** installed they glow in their own color under shaders, just like edge lights, hangar lights and signs.

## Gates and Check-in

<!-- Screenshot: check-in counter with a luggage scale on a belt line, gate sign hanging above -->

**Gate signs** hang from the ceiling and take text like airfield signs. **Check-in desks** join into a counter, and the **luggage scale** weighs each piece by what is packed inside: put it on by hand or let luggage roll through on belts. Above 23 kg the weight turns red, and a comparator reads it between the minimum and maximum you set.

## Hangar

<!-- Screenshot: hangar with a door made of panels and windows, floodlights shining down -->

**Hangar door panels** and **windows** join into one big door that you move with gantries, pistons or pulleys. **Hangar lights** throw a soft cone of light down to the floor.

## Cargo

<!-- Screenshot: LD11 locked on roller beds, two container ports in the wall next to it -->

**Cargo containers** are built from blocks in real ULD shapes: an LD2, LD3, LD11 or AMA, each block holding as much as an Item Vault block. A Physics Assembler takes the whole container as one piece, no glue needed. Slide it over **roller beds** to a **container lock** in the floor or the wall: the lock pulls it in like a magnet, lines it up and holds it until redstone lets go. **Container ports** in the wall then connect to the container like a Portable Storage Interface, and funnels or chutes at the port load and unload it.

## Traffic Cones

![Traffic Cones](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Airspace/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/traffic_cones.jpg)

Mark closed areas. Stack up to four per block, and they can be placed underwater too.

## Recipes

Simple blocks are crafted, everything else goes through Create: deploying, item application, mixing, sequenced assembly and mechanical crafting. All recipes show up in JEI, and most blocks have a **Ponder** scene (hold `W` over the item).

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**, **NeoForge**
- **Create 6.0.10** or newer
- **Create Aeronautics 1.3.0** or newer (with Simulated and Sable)
- Needed on both client and server
- Optional: **Curios** for wearing ear defenders in the head slot
- Optional: **Opal Light** for colored light under shaders
