# Create: Aviation

![Banner](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/banner.jpg)

**Airport accessories for Create.** Build a proper airfield around your aircraft: barrier posts for queue lines, terminal seats, taxiway signs, edge lights, a windsock that follows the wind and a rotating airport beacon.

Create: Aviation sits right next to Create and Create Aeronautics. It uses Create's own processes, Ponder scenes and wrench handling, so everything feels like part of Create.

---

## Barrier Posts

![Barrier Posts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/barrier_posts.jpg)

Connect two posts with a wrench and a retractable belt stretches between them. Each post holds several belts, perfect for queue lines. Recolour belts with dye.
Available in **industrial iron, andesite, brass, copper and train casing**.

## Terminal Seats

![Terminal Seats](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/terminal_seats.jpg)

Seats facing the same way join into rows, with armrests only at the ends. Sit down like on Create's seats, leashed mobs too, and they keep working on contraptions. 16 colours, recolour with dye.

## Airfield Signs

![Airfield Signs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/airfield_signs.jpg)

Location, direction and mandatory signs. Write on a whole row with a named **name tag**, a **clipboard** or a **Display Link**. The text spans the row, glows at night and is readable from both sides. Switch the sign type with a wrench.

## Edge Lights

![Edge Lights](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/edge_lights.jpg)

Light up with redstone. Redstone dust connects to them, so a single line beside a row of lights powers all of them. White, blue, green, red and yellow.

## Windsock

![Windsock](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/windsock.jpg)

Turns into the wind and rises with its strength. The wind is the same for every player and gets stronger with rain, thunderstorms and height.

## Airport Beacon

![Airport Beacon](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/airport_beacon.jpg)

Rotational force turns the head, redstone from the sides controls brightness and range of the light. Switch between the patterns of **land airports, water airports, heliports and military airports** with a wrench. Looking straight into a beam dazzles you for a moment (can be turned off in the client config).

## Traffic Cones

![Traffic Cones](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Piissedpanda/Create-Aviation/mc1.21.1/dev/docs/images/traffic_cones.jpg)

Mark closed areas. They can be placed underwater too.

---

## Recipes

Simple blocks are crafted, everything else goes through Create: deploying, item application, sequenced assembly and mechanical crafting. All recipes show up in JEI, and most blocks have a **Ponder** scene (hold `W` over the item).

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1**, **NeoForge**
- **Create 6.0.10** or newer
- Needed on both client and server

Works alongside **Create Aeronautics**.
